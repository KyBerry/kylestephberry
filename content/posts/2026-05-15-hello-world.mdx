# Hello, world

This site is where I think out loud about my work: building product interfaces where
design and engineering meet.

I'll write about motion and animation, design systems, accessibility, frontend architecture,
and the small decisions that make an interface feel good to use. Some posts will be long.
Others will be short notes on a single pattern.

If any of it is useful to you, great. If you want to talk, [reach out](/designs).
