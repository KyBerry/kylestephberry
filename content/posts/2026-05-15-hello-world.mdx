# Hello, world

This site is where I think out loud about the work I do — building product interfaces at the
intersection of design and engineering.

I'll write about motion and animation, design systems, accessibility, frontend architecture,
and the day-to-day decisions that separate good UI from great UI. Some posts will be long
explorations; others will be short notes on a single pattern or heuristic.

If any of it is useful to you, great. If you want to talk, [reach out](/designs).
