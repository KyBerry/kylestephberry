Most spring presets feel either floaty or twitchy. A short heuristic helps.

## The shape I aim for

Critically-damped springs settle without overshoot, which is fine for utility transitions
(menus, modals). Slightly underdamped springs overshoot about 5–10%, which is better for
hero motion, where the bounce reads as personality.

<Callout type="tip">
For UI surfaces (sheets, dialogs), pin damping near 0.9. For hero moments,
drop to 0.7 and bump stiffness to taste.
</Callout>

## Numbers I keep handy

- Sheet open: `{ stiffness: 380, damping: 36 }`
- Modal in: `{ stiffness: 260, damping: 26 }`
- Hero float: `{ stiffness: 120, damping: 14 }`

You can derive more by ear, but this set covers most of what I reach for.
