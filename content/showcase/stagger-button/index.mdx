A small piece of motion you can drop on any CTA. The hover staggers the
characters of the label individually — short enough not to feel laggy, long
enough to read as deliberate.

The ghost variant strips the fill and keeps the same character stagger; useful
inside contexts where a solid button would feel heavy.
